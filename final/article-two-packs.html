TWO PACKS: BLACK AND YELLOW

From the Journal - product news

Black Horse Vital Honey comes in two packs: the Black Pack and the Yellow Pack. Both are on this page, and both hold the same blend.

Inside each box are 24 sachets of 10 grams each. The blend is 100% natural honey with Royal Jelly, Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs, named in full on the box.

The difference between the two packs is the pack itself: black or yellow, with the same gold horse. Men pick the one they like the look of, or keep one of each.

Each pack is priced at $5 a box. Ordering is done on the Home page, where both packs sit with the full product details.

Whichever pack you choose, the box holds the same thing: 10 grams x 24 sachets of the Black Horse blend.
