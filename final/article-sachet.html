THE 10G SACHET, UP CLOSE

From the Journal - product news

The single sachet is the smallest part of Black Horse Vital Honey, and the part a man actually holds. Each gold sachet holds 10 grams of the blend.

Inside is 100% natural honey with Royal Jelly, Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs. The three ingredients are named on the box, and the same blend fills every sachet in the box.

A box holds 24 of these sachets, packed in rows. Nothing is loose and nothing is measured by hand; the portion is set at 10 grams before the sachet is sealed.

The sachets are the same in both packs. The Black Pack and the Yellow Pack differ in the box, not in what is inside it.

One box of 24 sachets is $5, in either pack, and both are on the Home page with the full product details.
