HARD MAN POWER

Hard Man Power is a premium men's wellness brand, built around one idea: a man should feel strong, confident and fully himself.

Made for Men

Hard Man Power is an adult men's brand. Everything about it, from the gold and black design to the products themselves, is made for men who take their energy, stamina and performance seriously. It is a brand that speaks plainly: no noise, no fuss, just a stronger you.

The brand offers two products. Royal Honey VIP is the classic honey product, presented in a yellow box of gold sachets. Black Bull Extreme is the bold one, a black spouted pouch with the gold bull on the front. Both carry the same promise the brand prints on its posters: natural energy for a stronger you.

Men choose Hard Man Power for the moments that matter. More energy through the day, enhanced stamina when it counts, better performance, and the confidence that comes with all three. That is the whole idea behind the brand, and it is why the posters carry the line: Feel the Difference.

The Two Products

Royal Honey VIP comes as a box of 12 sachets, each holding 20 grams. The box is the one shown across the brand's posters, beside the honeycomb and the honey dipper, under the words The Ultimate Power Source. It is the product for a man who likes to keep a supply at home, ready when he is.

Black Bull Extreme comes as a single spouted pouch of 22g (15g), marked Original, with the words Don't Quit ... printed under the bull. It is one ready serve, easy to carry, made for the man who wants his product simple and direct.

Both products are presented the same way everywhere the brand appears: black, gold, and the bull. Whichever one a man picks, he is picking the same standard: a premium men's wellness solution, made to be felt.
