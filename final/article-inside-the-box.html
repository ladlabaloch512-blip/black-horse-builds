INSIDE THE BOX OF 24

From the Journal - product news

One box of Black Horse Vital Honey holds 24 sachets. Each sachet holds 10 grams of the blend, so one full box holds 240 grams in total.

The sachets are gold, sealed one by one, and packed in rows inside the box. Each single sachet is one measured portion of the blend.

The blend inside every sachet is the same: 100% natural honey with Royal Jelly, Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs, the three ingredients named on the box.

The box comes in two packs, Black and Yellow. The pack colour changes; the 24 sachets inside do not.

Each box is priced at $5. Both packs, with the full product details and ordering, are on the Home page.
