ENERGY AND STAMINA: A DAILY ROUTINE FOR MEN

From the Journal - notes for the men who use it

Energy through a full day is mostly a routine, not a single moment. The men who stay steady from morning to night tend to do the simple things in the same order, every day.

Sleep comes first. A fixed wake-up time, a dark room, and screens put away before bed do more for the next day than any late-night push. Breakfast and water early, before the day starts asking for things.

Movement is the second piece. A walk, a stretch, or a short workout tells the body the day has started. Then work in blocks, with short breaks between them, instead of one long drain.

The evening matters as much as the morning. A proper meal, time with family, and a clear end to work keep the next morning strong.
