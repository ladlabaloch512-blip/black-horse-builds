Royal Jelly

ABOUT ROYAL JELLY

From the Journal - notes on the ingredients named on the box

Royal Jelly is one of the three ingredients named on the Black Horse Vital Honey box. The box lists it plainly, next to Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs, in a 100% natural honey base.

Royal Jelly is produced by worker bees and fed to the queen bee in the hive. Beekeepers collect it in small amounts, a little at a time.

In the Black Horse blend, Royal Jelly is named on the box as part of the blend. Every sachet holds 10 grams of the blend, and one box holds 24 sachets.

The rest of the box reads just as plainly: natural honey, Panax Ginseng, a root named on the box, and Rainforest Herbs, botanicals named on the box. That is the whole blend, named in full.

Black Horse Vital Honey comes in two packs, Black and Yellow. Each pack is one box: 10 grams x 24 sachets, 100% natural, at $5.
