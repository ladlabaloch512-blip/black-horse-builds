CONFIDENCE AND VITALITY: START WITH THE MORNING

From the Journal - notes for the men who use it

Confidence is built in small, repeated choices, and most of them happen before the day has properly started. The morning is where a man sets the tone for everything after it.

Start with the basics done well: a shower, a clean shave or a trimmed beard, and clothes chosen the night before. Looking ready is the first step to feeling ready.

Then give the day a shape. One page, three lines: the main task, the people to call, and the one thing that must not slip. A man who knows his plan walks into rooms differently.

Finish one small task early. An early win, even a small one, carries into the afternoon and the evening.
