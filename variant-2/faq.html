What is Black Horse Vital Honey?−

Black Horse Vital Honey is a 100% natural honey blend with Royal Jelly, Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs. It comes in sachets of 10 grams each.

One box holds 24 sachets, and each sachet is 10 grams.

What are the main ingredients?+

The blend is made with Royal Jelly, Panax Ginseng and Rainforest Herbs, in a 100% natural honey base.
